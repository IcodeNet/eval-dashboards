# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge that everyone participating in the @icodenet/eval-dashboards project and community will be treated with respect and dignity, free from harassment, discrimination, or intimidation of any kind.

## Our Values

We believe in:

- **Openness** — We welcome ideas, feedback, and diverse perspectives from all backgrounds
- **Inclusivity** — Everyone is welcome regardless of experience level, identity, or background
- **Collaboration** — We value constructive dialogue and working together toward shared goals
- **Respect** — We respect each other's time, expertise, and contributions
- **Integrity** — We act with honesty and take responsibility for our words and actions

## Expected Behavior

All participants in this community are expected to:

- Use inclusive and welcoming language
- Be respectful of differing opinions and experiences
- Give and receive constructive criticism gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the community
- Show empathy toward other community members
- Help create a safe and welcoming environment for all

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation based on identity (race, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age, religion, etc.)
- Offensive language or imagery, including slurs
- Deliberate intimidation, threats, or stalking
- Non-consensual physical contact or sexual advances
- Unwanted solicitation or spam
- Sustained disruption of conversations or meetings
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Any conduct that would be considered unprofessional in a workplace setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All interactions in GitHub repositories (issues, pull requests, discussions)
- Comments on code and documentation
- Interactions at community events or online meetings
- Any space where someone represents the @icodenet/eval-dashboards project or community

## Enforcement

### Reporting

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainers at **byronth@gmail.com** with:

- Description of the incident
- Names of those involved (if known)
- Context and links (if applicable)
- Any additional information that would help us understand the situation

All reports will be handled confidentially and investigated promptly.

### Response

Upon receiving a report, maintainers will:

1. Acknowledge the report within 48 hours
2. Investigate the incident fairly and promptly
3. Take appropriate action, which may include:
   - Asking the person to stop the behavior
   - Issuing a formal warning
   - Temporary or permanent removal from the community
   - Banning from contributing to the project

The person reported will have an opportunity to respond to the allegation before final action is taken.

## Consequences

Participants who violate this Code of Conduct may face:

- **Warning** — A clear statement that the behavior is unacceptable
- **Temporary suspension** — Removal from the community for a defined period
- **Permanent removal** — Exclusion from the project and community indefinitely

The severity of the response depends on the nature and context of the violation.

## Acknowledgment

This Code of Conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) and adapted for the needs of the @icodenet/eval-dashboards community.

## Questions or Concerns?

If you have questions about this Code of Conduct or want to report a concern, please contact **byronth@gmail.com**.
